Open source project

Volt Maps is a community mirror of the Reborn WebGL player. No account, no trackers, no data collected: everything runs on your machine, fully offline.

Maps come from the official game content and remain the property of their respective owners.

Support

The mirror is free and will stay that way. If it serves you, a donation helps keep it alive.
