Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Direct answers about maps, saves, browsers, Rank, privacy and contact.

01

Do I need an account?

Not to open public maps. Discord may be required for identity features, run submission and profile management.

02

Where is progress stored?

Usually in the browser storage used to open the map. Clearing site data or changing devices can remove unexported saves.

03

Which browsers work?

Modern browsers with WebGL and WebAssembly generally work best. Memory, GPU, network blocks and build compatibility also matter.

04

Are there ads?

Yes. The site displays fixed banners at the top of public pages and a short step before opening each map. No ad is started during gameplay.

05

Is the project official?

No. It is an independent community project; see the legal notice for trademark and affiliation details.

06

How do I request help or removal?

Contact tavvkkj on Discord with the URL, a clear description and the relevant supporting information.
